Section 01
What is PreOne?

PreOne is an Enterprise Preschool Operating System — a multi-tenant SaaS platform where the PreOne team first onboards client schools, and each school then runs its entire operation in one place. Every school (tenant) has fully isolated data arranged as Tenant → Branch → Classroom → Student. The UI follows the Windows Shell design system: a top header shows your context, and a bottom taskbar with the Start button navigates between modules, in light or dark theme.

Section 03
The Complete Flow — Client to Classroom
1

Client Onboarding

Platform Admin
  1. Log in as platform@preone.in — you land on the Platform Console (/onboard) listing every onboarded client.
  2. Click New Tenant → enter school name, unique code, city, subscription plan and limits (max branches / students).
  3. The school is created in PENDING_ACTIVATION with an onboarding-step tracker; the console shows students, branches and user counts live.
  4. Create the main branch and the OWNER account, then hand the credentials to the client — their setup begins.
  5. Monitor, activate or suspend the tenant any time from the same console.
2

School Setup

Owner / Principal
  1. Log in as the owner → complete school profile, branding and the academic session in Settings.
  2. In Academics, create classrooms for the session (Nursery, KG-1, KG-2, Daycare…).
  3. In Finance, define Fee Plans — heads, amounts and frequency (monthly / term / annual).
  4. In Settings → Users, invite staff and assign roles — principal, teachers, accounts, reception.
3

Daily Operations

All Staff
  1. Admissions: Reception captures a lead → converts it to an application → verifies documents → Principal approves → the child becomes an enrolled student automatically.
  2. Attendance: teachers mark their classroom daily (Present / Absent / Late / Half-day); Principal reviews and approves.
  3. Finance: Accounts generates invoices from fee plans → records payments → receipts issue instantly; the dashboard tracks collection rate.
  4. Academics + Communication: teachers publish observations to the timeline; announcements broadcast to staff and parents.
Section 04
Module-by-Module Quick Reference

Open any module from the Start menu (bottom taskbar). Each module below lists what it does and the actions that matter most in daily use.

Dashboard /app

Live KPIs — total and active students, today's attendance %, fee billed vs collected with overdue amount, pending admissions, and a 7-day attendance trend.

Students /app/students

Full student roster with search. Open a profile for guardians, classroom, fee invoices and the activity timeline. Enrolments come from approved applications.

Admissions /app/admissions

Pipeline of applications: Submitted → Document Pending → Verified → Under Review → Approved / Rejected. Approving enrols the child as a student.

Attendance /app/attendance

Pick a classroom and date, mark each child Present / Absent / Late / Half-day. Summary percentages per class; Principal approval on marked days.

Academics /app/academics

Classroom observations — teachers draft, then publish. Published entries flow to the student timeline so parents can follow learning progress.

Finance /app/finance

Fee plans → invoice generation → payment recording → receipts. Amounts tracked in paise with discounts and tax; overdue invoices flagged automatically.

Communication /app/communication

Announcements targeted at staff or parents — circulars, event notices, holiday reminders. Recent posts also surface on the dashboard.

Timeline /app/timeline

Chronological school activity feed — admissions events, attendance, fee payments, published observations — a single audit-friendly story.

Audit /app/audit

Immutable log of every sensitive action: logins, approvals, fee changes, deletes. Filter by action or entity — your compliance record lives here.

Settings /app/settings

School profile and branding, academic sessions, and user management — invite staff, assign the 8 roles, deactivate access when someone leaves.

Section 05
Pro Tips
1

Navigate with the Start button in the bottom taskbar — every module is one click away, and the top header always shows your school and page context.

2

Roles are strict. If you can't see a module, your role doesn't include it — ask the Owner (Settings → Users) for the right role rather than sharing logins.

3

Switch themes from the header — dark mode ("Deep Space") is easier on the eyes for evening front-desk shifts.

4

Money is always exact — amounts are stored in paise, so invoices, payments and receipts always reconcile to the paisa.

5

Nothing vanishes. Deleted records are soft-deleted and every sensitive action is audit-logged — check the Audit module if something looks off.
